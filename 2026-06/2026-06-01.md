### [Choosing the right MCMC sampler: a systematic benchmark of gradient-free methods](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30412v1)

Colin M. Poppelaars, Marcel P. van Daalen
**Primary category:** astro-ph.IM (cross-listed: astro-ph.CO, astro-ph.GA)

Benchmarks a wide set of gradient-free ensemble MCMC samplers — stretch, walk, differential evolution (DE), snooker, and two novel variants (modified stretch and a PCA-stretch) — against standard Metropolis-Hastings using controlled toy posteriors (Rosenbrock, Neal's funnel, multimodal Gaussian landscapes up to 8D). Key metrics are ergodicity, convergence speed, and robustness. The DE sampler with 25% scaling outperforms MH consistently across all test cases. Novel PCA-based variants show promise but require further validation in higher dimensions.

### [Statistical imprints of wave-like dark matter on multiply-imaged galaxies in strong cluster lenses from JWST](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30649v1)

Nino Ephremidze, Daniel Gilman, Cora Dvorkin
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Develops a statistical probe of wave-like (fuzzy/ultralight) dark matter using the 2D residual power spectrum P_δ(k) of JWST strong cluster lens images, measured from mock multiply-imaged galaxies. The power spectrum encodes the population-level lensing imprint of DM substructure without resolving individual subhalos. For boson masses around 10^−22 eV, subhalo suppression wins over wave-interference enhancement, pushing P_δ(k) below CDM; at 10^−23 eV the wave fluctuations dominate and P_δ(k) exceeds CDM. The signal is a broadband power-law modification rather than a sharp spectral feature, due to projection along the line of sight.

### [The MeerKAT Thousand-Pulsar Polarisation Array II: Searches for Ultralight Axion-Like Dark Matter](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31024v1)

Zi-Yan Yuwen, Michael Sarkis, Yin-Zhe Ma et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.HE (cross-listed: astro-ph.CO)

Constructs a Pulsar Polarisation Array from 50 high-SNR pulsars in the MeerKAT Thousand Pulsar Array programme, searching for axion-like dark matter via oscillations of the Faraday rotation measure (Chern-Simons coupling). Six-year baseline yields no detection over 10^−23 – 10^−20 eV. Establishes new most-sensitive upper limits: g_{aγγ} ≲ 10^−14 – 3×10^−13 GeV^−1 across the mass range, improving on previous pulsar limits.

### [Observing the Peculiar Acceleration of our Solar System with Quasar Proper Motions](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30367v1)

Phu Huy Nguyen, Calum Murray
**Primary category:** astro-ph.GA (cross-listed: astro-ph.CO)

Measures the Solar System's peculiar acceleration via the proper-motion dipole of ~1.2M Gaia EDR3 quasars, using the angular power spectrum of the proper-motion field (pseudo-C_ℓ formalism) and SBI to jointly constrain the dipole and higher-multipole contamination. SBI is used to marginalize over the Gaia scanning-law systematics (which are highly non-trivial). Cross-correlates with Gaia scanning-law templates to separate systematics from signal. Acceleration estimate is consistent with Galactic-centre expectation.

### [(The) Wiggles going non-linear](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30953v1)

Nathan Cohen, Jan Hamann
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Validates and extends a semi-analytic Gaussian damping model for the non-linear suppression of primordial power spectrum features (logarithmic and linear wiggles) in the late-time matter power spectrum over 0.05–0.6 h/Mpc. Uses matched feature/featureless N-body simulation pairs to calibrate the damping envelope Σ(ω, z), then wraps it in a Gaussian Process emulator that provides a continuous, uncertainty-quantified prediction usable inside a likelihood. The residual error stays below 5×10^−3 across the target parameter space, meeting percent-level accuracy requirements for current LSS surveys.

### [Gravothermal Collapse: Robust Against Baryonic Feedback](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31335v1)

Demao Kong, Hai-Bo Yu
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO (cross-listed: astro-ph.GA, hep-ph)

Stress-tests gravothermal collapse in SIDM halos against extreme baryonic feedback using a semi-analytic oscillating-potential model in controlled N-body simulations. For high-concentration halos (short SIDM thermalization timescale), collapse is only mildly delayed and never stalled even under extreme feedback; for median-concentration halos collapse can be delayed but always resumes, with final central densities sensitive to the episodic feedback history. Establishes gravothermal collapse as a robust prediction of DM self-interactions, not erasable by baryonic processes.

### [Euclid preparation. Probing galaxy evolution within cosmic voids in Euclid-like simulations](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30440v1)

Euclid Collaboration, G. Papini, O. Cucciati et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.GA

Uses the GAEA semi-analytic model run on an Euclid-like lightcone (GAEA-LVIII) to study how galaxy properties — stellar mass, sSFR, bulge-to-total ratio, merger history — depend on distance from void centres, spanning z = 0–1.5. Void galaxies are less massive, more star-forming, and disk-like relative to field galaxies at fixed stellar mass, consistent with the expected suppression of environmental processes. Merger rates are lower in voids.

### [Mapping the Universe as a Bianchi I cosmology with Gaia data](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30579v1)

Valeri V. Makarov, Asta Heinesen, Thomas Schücker
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Fits the full proper-motion field of 1.2M Gaia DR3 quasars (z = 0.5–3, five non-overlapping redshift bins) with vector spherical harmonics up to degree 2, using an axisymmetric Bianchi I model as a phenomenological test of the cosmological principle. Finds moderate evidence (2–3σ) for a redshift-dependent dipole amplitude that cannot be explained by the Solar System's constant acceleration alone — consistent with either an unaccounted Gaia systematic or a genuine cosmological anisotropy signature.

### [Gravitational Waves from hybrid defects as probe of Flavor symmetry breaking: Machine-Learning Approach](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31600v1)

Anish Ghoshal, Ilia Gogoladze, Amit Tiwari
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO (cross-listed: hep-ph, hep-th)

Proposes that a network of cosmic strings bounded by domain walls, arising from gauged U(1)_F flavor symmetry breaking followed by Z_2 breaking that accommodates dark matter, generates a stochastic GW background detectable by LISA/ET/SKA. The GW spectrum has a distinctive infrared frequency slope distinguishing it from pure cosmic string backgrounds. An ML classifier is trained to distinguish this hybrid-defect spectrum from other cosmic-defect models using the multi-detector signal-to-noise ratio.

### [The EPS Research Astro-RAG Platform: A Unified Open-Science Infrastructure for Cross-Epoch Astrophysical Kinematic Analysis, LLM-Assisted Research Workflows, and Educational Outreach](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30384v1)

David C. Flynn
**Primary category:** astro-ph.IM (cross-listed: astro-ph.GA)

Describes an open-science platform (v1.0) providing four machine-readable astrophysical corpora (772 objects spanning z = 0 to z ~ 6: HI rotation curves, Milky Way globular clusters, high-z ALMA [CII] kinematics) in a common JSON/CSV/JSONL schema, 120 verified executable Jupyter notebooks, and a documented roadmap for LLM-assisted RAG research workflows. Includes a QuickStart reproducibility pathway and a high-school outreach track. The unifying scientific claim is a "cross-epoch omega sign reversal" in galaxy spin (positive at z=0, negative at z~5).
