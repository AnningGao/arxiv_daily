## Tier 2 — Adjacent / useful context

### [21cmEMUv3: a hybrid diffusion-LSTM emulator of 21cmFAST summary observables](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00219v1)
Daniela Breitman, Andrei Mesinger, Steven G. Murray et al. | **astro-ph.CO** (cross-listed cs.LG)

Presents a new emulator of 21cmFAST conditioned on 11 astrophysical and cosmological parameters, trained to jointly predict 7 summary observables: a score-based diffusion model handles the 2D cylindrical 21-cm power spectrum, LSTM networks handle five time-evolving scalar observables (global signal, IGM temperature, neutral fraction, spin temperature, ionized fraction), and a feed-forward network handles the CMB optical depth. Achieves sub-percent median fractional error across all outputs, with the 2D power spectrum emulated at better accuracy than previous single-architecture approaches. This is the first emulator to combine a generative probabilistic model (diffusion) with sequence models (LSTMs) for cosmic dawn inference, and feeds directly into simulation-based inference pipelines.

### [Generative Diffusion Priors for 3D Mapping of the Dark Universe](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00803v1)
Brandon Zhao, Diana Scognamiglio, Olivier Doré et al. | **astro-ph.CO** (cross-listed cs.CV, cs.LG)

Introduces a simulation-trained diffusion model as a prior for Bayesian 3D dark matter reconstruction from 2D weak-lensing shear maps — an ill-posed inverse problem because only a single line-of-sight is available and galaxy distances are uncertain. The diffusion sampler generates posterior samples of the full 3D matter field given noisy shear observations; it substantially outperforms Wiener filter and neural ensemble baselines in 3D/2D power spectrum fidelity and remains robust under cosmology mismatch. The authors also release the first 3D dark matter lightcone dataset tailored for WL mass mapping.

### [Optimal Transport Reconstruction of Biased Tracers in Primordial Non-Gaussian Fields](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01529v1)
Farnik Nikakhtar, Ravi K. Sheth, Nikhil Padmanabhan et al. | **astro-ph.CO**

Extends optimal transport (OT) initial-condition reconstruction, previously validated only for Gaussian initial conditions, to local-type primordial non-Gaussian fields (fNL). The OT displacement-field estimator remains accurate even in non-Gaussian cosmologies and can detect the fNL-induced signature in the large-scale displacement field of biased tracers (galaxies or quasars). This is timely: DESI and future surveys are targeting primordial non-Gaussianity via scale-dependent bias, and this provides a complementary geometric reconstruction path.

### [Mergers Matter: Gravothermal Collapse in Dwarf Halos with Self-Interacting Dark Matter](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02566v1)
Maya Silverman, Abdelaziz Hussein, Arpit Arora et al. | **astro-ph.GA** (cross-listed astro-ph.CO, hep-ph)

Runs six cosmological zoom-in simulations of ~10^10 M_sun halos with a large SIDM cross section (σ/m = 70 cm²/g) that are expected to undergo gravothermal core collapse within a Hubble time in isolation. Finds that mergers inject orbital kinetic energy that can delay or prevent core collapse: three halos with quiescent histories collapse, three with active merger histories do not and instead maintain lower central densities. The underlying mechanism is that high cross sections make subhalo–halo particle interactions efficient enough to transport energy outward, heating the core.

### [It's Not Just Star Formation: A trend of low dark matter densities in the Andromeda dwarf galaxy system](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00221v1)
Connor S. Pickett, Michelle L. M. Collins, Justin I. Read et al. | **astro-ph.GA**

Dynamically mass-models seven more M31 dwarf spheroidals (And I, III, V, VII, IX, XXXI, XXXII) using Gravsphere, expanding the M31 sample to 11 modeled dwarfs. All seven show central DM densities lower than CDM predictions and systematically lower than their MW counterpart dwarfs — reinforcing a trend identified in earlier M31 work. Stellar feedback alone cannot explain the difference (most are quenched ultra-faint systems with minimal star formation), pointing to either environmental effects from M31 or a genuine issue with CDM predictions at the low-mass end.

### [Accurate inner stellar density slopes from projected surface densities in galaxies](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01956v1)
Jorge Sanchez Almeida | **astro-ph.GA** (cross-listed astro-ph.CO)

Derives a closed-form expression to infer the central 3D stellar density slope ρ'(0) directly from radial derivatives of the projected surface density Σ(R), without explicit Abel deprojection — avoiding the noise-amplifying instability of standard deprojection. Shows that all projected profiles have the same central functional form regardless of the underlying 3D density, so derivatives of Σ(R) can be extrapolated to R=0 using large-radius constraints. Applied to six ultra-faint dwarf galaxies, all show Σ(R) shapes consistent with stellar cores (ρ'[0] ≈ 0).

### [CSST large-scale structure analysis pipeline: IV. Cosmic Voids Identified from Galaxy Group Samples](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01907v1)
Yingxiao Song, Xiaohu Yang, Yan Gong et al. | **astro-ph.CO** (cross-listed astro-ph.GA)

Proposes using voids identified in galaxy *group* catalogs (rather than individual galaxies) as LSS probes for the Chinese Space Station Telescope, arguing that group-based voids allow simpler theoretical modeling because groups directly trace halos. Uses CSST mock galaxy redshift surveys with 100% and ~30% spectroscopic completeness to characterize group-void statistics. Demonstrates competitive cosmological constraints on Ωm and σ8 from the group-void size function.

### [The sound of dynamical dark energy and modified gravity](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00411v1)
João Rebouças, Guilherme Brando, Felipe T. Falciano et al. | **astro-ph.CO**

Investigates whether competing dynamical dark energy models — which all reproduce the w0-wa signal preferred by DESI DR2 BAO — can be distinguished by the behavior of their perturbations, controlled by the effective sound speed c_s²(k,a). Tests several modified gravity scenarios and shows that differences in the matter power spectrum (at the ~1% level) and CMB temperature/polarization spectra are measurable with near-future experiments. 21-cm line-intensity mapping is identified as particularly powerful for breaking degeneracies.

### [Decaying Dark Matter as a Possible Solution for Cosmological Tensions](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00529v1)
Yaman Acharya, Ryan E. Johnson | **astro-ph.CO** (cross-listed hep-ph)

A preliminary study of whether a two-body decaying dark matter model (DDM → warm daughter + massless species) can alleviate the S8 tension by suppressing late-time structure growth. Compares DDM predictions against DES Y1 galaxy–lensing cross-correlations and finds that the DDM suppression is quantitatively consistent with the level needed to reconcile weak-lensing and CMB-derived σ8. The authors explicitly label this a preliminary investigation requiring stronger data constraints.

### [The Murchison Widefield Array Phase III upgrade](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01644v1)
S. J. Tingay, M. Johnston-Hollitt, R. B. Wayth et al. | **astro-ph.IM** (cross-listed astro-ph.CO)

Describes the completed Phase III upgrade of the MWA: new digital receivers now support full correlation of all 256 antenna tiles simultaneously, doubling sensitivity and quadrupling the number of baselines compared to Phase II (128 tiles). The upgrade is explicitly optimized for Epoch of Reionization power spectrum observations in the 21-cm line. The paper details the receiver hardware, signal chain, and deployment, and discusses prospects for improved EoR constraints.

### [The Information Content of Quasar Variability Light Curves: How Well Can we Infer Stochastic Model Parameters?](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01496v1)
Brendon J. Brewer, Geraint F. Lewis, Xiang Yu et al. | **astro-ph.GA** (cross-listed physics.data-an, stat.AP)

Demonstrates a systematic problem with standard DRW (damped random walk) fits to quasar variability light curves: fixing the mean parameter μ (the common practice) yields overconfident and biased posterior estimates of the characteristic timescale τ. Marginalizing over μ is essential but reveals that τ is weakly constrained by typical light curves — the information content is genuinely low. Develops a Bayesian treatment showing this identifiability issue persists even with well-sampled data.

Marco Chiaberge, Elias Stengel-Eskin, Massimo Stiavelli et al. | **astro-ph.GA** (cross-listed astro-ph.IM)

Tests an ensemble of 15 VLM configurations (4 architectures: Gemma-4 E2B, Gemma-4 E4B, Qwen2.5-VL, Qwen3-VL; up to 4 prompt engineering strategies) combined via Bayesian statistical framework for galaxy merger vs. non-merger morphological classification. Achieves 83.3% accuracy on confident classifications (p_M ≥ 0.8 or ≤ 0.2), comparable to trained human experts at 85%, with 5 vs. 6 misclassifications. Requires no fine-tuning — entirely zero-shot.

### [Signatures of Accreting Black Holes in Line Intensity Mapping](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01603v1)
Erika Ogata, Kana Moriwaki, Adam Lidz et al. | **astro-ph.GA**

Uses IllustrisTNG to quantify how AGN/BH-powered emission contributes to Hα and HeII 1640 Å line-intensity mapping signals at z=4–6. Finds BH-powered emission dominates the HeII LIM signal and contributes 30–40% of the mean Hα intensity at z>4. Auto- and cross-power spectra as well as voxel intensity distributions are all significantly modified by AGN contributions. Ignoring BH emission would bias cosmological parameter inference from future LIM surveys.

## Tier 3 — Outside my area but notable

### [JWST's Little Red Dots as collapsed Supermassive Dark Stars](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02539v1)
Cosmin Ilie | **astro-ph.CO** (cross-listed gr-qc, hep-ph)

Proposes that JWST's "Little Red Dots" (LRDs) — compact, red, high-luminosity objects at z~4–8 with mysterious spectral properties — could be quasi-star-like systems formed from the collapse of supermassive dark stars (SMDSs) powered by WIMP dark matter annihilation rather than nuclear burning. The SMDS pathway relaxes several fine-tuned constraints of the standard supermassive-star route (strong Lyman-Werner backgrounds, extreme gas inflow rates) because WIMPs provide an internal energy source that sustains the star's envelope independently of environment. The resulting quasi-star properties (mass, luminosity, spectral shape) match observed LRD characteristics.

### [OCEANS of Absorption: High-resolution NIRSpec Spectroscopy Reveals Diverse Balmer-line Absorption in Little Red Dots](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00258v1)
Kelcey Davis, Madisyn Brooks, Raymond C. Simons et al. | **astro-ph.GA**

Presents high-resolution (R~2700) JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy of 10 LRDs with Hα coverage at z=3–7 from the OCEANS program in the CEERS/EGS field. Detects Balmer-line absorption in 4 of 10 (40%), substantially higher than the 10–20% reported by lower-resolution surveys. All four absorbers have their Balmer absorption resolved at high resolution for the first time; two are new detections. The broad Hα + absorption profile suggests the absorbing gas is dense and compact, consistent with a massive star-forming or AGN host seen through its own gas.
