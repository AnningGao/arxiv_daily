## Tier 1 — Highly relevant

### [The free-streaming length of dark matter from JWST observations of 28 strong gravitational lenses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05277)

D. Gilman, A. M. Nierenberg, T. Treu et al. (K. N. Abazajian, K. K. Rogers among co-authors)
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO | also: astro-ph.GA

JWST quadrupole-lens imaging of 28 quad systems is used to simultaneously reconstruct extended arcs plus image magnifications, significantly improving sensitivity to DM substructure compared to image-positions-only analyses. The measurement rules out CDM deviations on halo scales above $10^{7.2}$–$10^{7.4}\,M_\odot$, translating to free-streaming length upper limits $\lambda_\mathrm{FS} < 6$–$7\,\mathrm{kpc}$ and thermal-relic WDM particle lower limits $m_\mathrm{therm} > 6.5$–$7.4\,\mathrm{keV}$ — the strongest strong-lensing constraints to date. A CDM subhalo projected mass of $1.7^{+2.6}_{-1.2}\times10^7\,M_\odot\,\mathrm{kpc}^{-2}$ is measured under the CDM assumption.

### [The Atacama Cosmology Telescope: Probing new signatures of ultralight axions with gravitational lensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06410)

A. Laguë, K. K. Rogers, M. S. Madhavacheril, J. R. Bond, E. Calabrese et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO | also: gr-qc

Fits CMB lensing power spectra from Planck, ACT, and SPT-3G using a simulation-calibrated nonlinear clustering model for ultralight axions (ULAs), folded into the `cosmopower` neural emulator framework for fast likelihood evaluation. Derives the strongest current constraints on ULAs with masses $10^{-26}\,\mathrm{eV} \leq m_a \leq 10^{-24.5}\,\mathrm{eV}$: ULAs at $10^{-26}\,\mathrm{eV}$ compose less than 1.5% of DM, and $10^{-25}\,\mathrm{eV}$ axions compose less than a few percent. A mild ($2\sigma$) preference for non-zero ULA density around $m_a \approx 10^{-24.7}\,\mathrm{eV}$ is identified via nested sampling, driven by excess CMB lensing power at $L\sim1000$.

## Tier 2 — Adjacent / useful context

### [Strong [OIII]+Hβ emitters dominated the ionizing budget at z∼7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05323)

I. Wold, S. Malhotra, J. Rhoads
**Primary category:** astro-ph.GA

Uses JWST UNCOVER F410M medium-band imaging of the Abell 2744 field — reaching an order of magnitude deeper than previous WFSS OIII studies — to identify the deepest spectroscopically confirmed sample of strong OIII+Hβ emitters at $z\sim7$. NIRSpec follow-up confirms 72% of targets; these galaxies have negligible dust, metallicities of 1–10% solar, and escape fractions around 20%. The population constitutes ~60% of galaxies by number density and contributes ~70% of the required ionizing budget at $z=7$, suggesting ordinary galaxy populations can close the reionization photon budget without exotic sources.

### [Research Note: Ghostly DLAs in SDSS DR16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06270)

P. Petitjean
**Primary category:** astro-ph.GA

Follow-up research note revisiting proximate damped Lyman-α absorbers (PDLAs) and ghostly DLA systems in SDSS DR16 quasar spectra. Provides a revised catalogue of 187 ghostly DLA candidates (more than doubling the previously known sample), and uses stacked spectra to show a continuous sequence from ordinary DLAs through DLA-Cor* to ghostly systems, characterized by increasing high-ionization species (OVI, NV, CIV), broader velocity spreads, and stronger Si II* excited-state absorption. The evidence points to compact, dense outflowing clouds near the AGN illuminated by intense ionizing radiation.

### [A Decade to Map the Diffuse Universe: FRB-QSO Pairs with HST/COS Spectroscopy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05310)

J. Werk, M. McQuinn, J. X. Prochaska, S. Simha, R. Bordoloi et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.GA

White paper proposing a decade-long HST/COS program to observe UV-bright QSOs projected near arcsecond-localized FRBs. Each FRB provides integrated electron column (DM), RM, and scattering constraints independent of gas phase; the QSO sight line provides resolved absorption-line signatures of specific ions, temperatures, and velocities. Together they enable strong constraints on the phases, mass distribution, and magnetic structure of the diffuse universe — circumgalactic and intergalactic medium.

### [Reconstructing dark energy with fewer assumptions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05853)

D. A. Kessler, E. Di Valentino, L. A. Escamilla, D. Huterer
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Performs assumption-minimal, bin-by-bin reconstruction of dark energy density and equation of state across 7 redshift bins from $z=0$ to $z=4.2$ using DESI BAO + SDSS BAO + Pantheon+/Union3.1/DES-Dovekie SNe. Avoids time-correlation priors that would smooth the evolution. All data combinations consistently show: dark energy density rising to a local maximum then declining, with an equation of state crossing $w=-1$ twice ($w>-1$ at intermediate redshifts, $w<-1$ at late times), driven primarily by the tension between DESI's high-$z$ BAO and SN data.

### [The DESI results impact the local determination of H₀](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05358)

M. S. Turner, D. Huterer
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Short letter showing that if DESI's preferred $w_0w_a$ dark energy models are used to analyze local distance measurements, the inferred Hubble constant drops by up to 2.5 km/s/Mpc relative to $\Lambda$CDM. With DESI+CMB+SNe constraints, the downward shift is $0.5\pm0.1$ km/s/Mpc — non-negligible at the level of the Hubble tension.

### [The quenching time and timescale distribution of z∼2 quiescent galaxies from precise colour distribution analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05971)

V. Wild, H.-H. Leung, A. Carnall, M. Skarbinski
**Primary category:** astro-ph.GA

Uses simulation-based inference (SBI) directly on the broad-band photometric colour distribution of massive galaxies ($\log M_*/M_\odot > 10.3$) at $1.7 < z < 2$ to constrain the joint distribution of formation times, quenching times, and quenching timescales. Demonstrates that a double-power-law SFH distribution accurately fits the SED shape distribution. Measures quenched fraction $0.24\pm0.02$, with rapid rise 2.5 Gyr after the Big Bang.

## Tier 3 — Outside my area but notable

### [On the potential for inhomogeneities to mimic an evolving dark energy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05479)

H. J. Macpherson, G. Valogiannis
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO | also: gr-qc

Uses full nonlinear general-relativistic ray tracing through $N$-body simulations to infer $w_0w_a$ dark energy parameters for 20 synthetic observers in a $\Lambda$CDM universe with realistic large-scale structure. Finds that one observer in 20 can spuriously infer $w_0, w_a$ values consistent with the DESI signal at $2\sigma$ — residing in an over-dense environment that shifts the inferred distance-redshift relation.

### [Wave-Tide Locking in Thin Stellar Streams: A Phenomenological Mass Spectrometer for an Intermediate Ultralight Axion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06394)

(hep-ph; single author)
**Primary category:** hep-ph

Derives an analytic "wave-tide locking theorem": if a stellar stream's width equals the axion de Broglie wavelength, the dark core's tidal radius automatically exceeds the gravitational Bohr radius by a factor of 25–40. This links stream width, de Broglie wavelength, and core survival into a no-simulation mass estimator for intermediate ULAs ($m_a \sim 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{eV}$). Applied to existing stream data to produce estimated mass ranges.

## Tier 4 — Meta-research about the field

### [Toward decision-aware AI for LSST-scale time-domain astronomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05285)

(multiple authors; Rubin/LSST collaboration context)
**Primary category:** astro-ph.HE

Argues that LSST's $10^7$ nightly alerts cross a qualitative threshold where static ML classifiers are insufficient: what is needed is a system that maintains an evolving belief state over physical properties, evaluates informational consequences of follow-up actions against scientific objectives, and proposes interventions at machine latency. Proposes a three-layer architecture: foundation models for perception, world models for counterfactual reasoning, and decision-theoretic policies for follow-up allocation.
