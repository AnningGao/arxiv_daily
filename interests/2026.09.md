## Executive profile

This is a **Lyman-α forest cosmology** library. Everything radiates out from one organizing question: *how do you extract cosmological and astrophysical information from the high-redshift intergalactic medium as seen in quasar absorption spectra*, now firmly in the DESI DR2 era. The owner is an observational/data-driven cosmologist working at the meeting point of large-scale-structure cosmology, IGM/reionization astrophysics, fundamental-physics constraints (dark matter, neutrinos), and inference methodology.

What the summer 2026 additions say (the freshest signal, May–August 2026):

1. **The IGM thermal state has become a live sub-focus.** A coherent new cluster on the temperature–density relation and thermal history appeared: Šoltinský et al. (2026) on the impact of the IGM thermal state on the Lyα 3D flux power spectrum, McQuinn & Upton Sanderbeck (2016) on the T–ρ relation, Walther et al. (2019) on thermal evolution from the P1D, Hiss et al. (2018) on Voigt-profile T–ρ measurements, Puchwein et al. (2015) on UV-background photoheating, and McQuinn et al. (2011) on large-scale temperature/intensity fluctuations — plus classical theory (Hui, Gnedin & Zhang 1997 on density peaks and the column density distribution) and the Rybicki & Lightman radiative-processes textbook.
2. **DESI DR2 Lyα analyses are current working material.** Newly added: the DESI DR2 Lyα forest full-shape validation (Herbold et al. 2026), high-z quasar clustering with DESI DR2 (Charles et al. 2026), the DESI 2024 BAL-masking validation (Martini et al. 2025), and reionization relics in the eBOSS DR16 Lyα correlations (Zheng et al. 2026).
3. **The EFT-of-the-forest thread continues:** the hybrid simulation+EFT model of the Lyα forest field (de Belsunce, Hadzhiyska & Ivanov 2026) and the analytic compression of the forest EFT (Karaçaylı et al. 2026).
4. **Quasar spectra & continuum modelling is an emerging astronomy-flavored interest:** a Gaussian-process generative model for quasar spectra (Eilers et al. 2022) together with the underlying GP latent-variable machinery, AGNFormer (probabilistic transformer reconstruction of AGN spectra, 2026), the SDSS Reverberation Mapping key results, type-2 quasar demographics, and Balmer-absorption AGN/little red dots. This connects directly to the QSO-continuum systematic in forest measurements.
5. **An active "quasar transformer" project (the QGPT collection) reveals the current ML direction.** The summer ML additions are not random: transformer interpretability and attention analysis (Chefer et al., Abnar & Zuidema, "Attention is (not) Explanation", sparse autoencoders), self-supervised/contrastive representation learning (SimCLR, BYOL, MoCo, iBOT), time-series representation learning (TS2Vec, ContiFormer), spectral foundation models (AION-1, AstroCLIP, SpecPT, SpecCLIP, universal spectral tokenization, Leung & Bovy), quasar-continuum ML (LyCAN, SpenderQ, Quasar Factor Analysis, GP DLA detection), null-space projection/knowledge editing, and deep-learning theory (double descent, benign overfitting). These all feed a self-supervised transformer model of quasar spectra. ML papers matching these threads are in-scope even without an astronomy application.

The forward direction: **DESI DR2-era Lyman-α cosmology (P1D, P3D/full-shape, BAO) with careful IGM astrophysics — thermal state, reionization relics, quasar continua — as the systematics frontier, plus a self-supervised transformer/foundation-model project for quasar spectra.**

## 1. Lyman-α forest cosmology — *primary focus*

Organized by **statistic** (P1D, P3D, correlation functions), **estimator/technique**, **measurement vs. theory/simulation**, and **application** (cosmological parameters, dark matter, reionization).

**Scope & sub-themes:**
- **1D flux power spectrum (P1D)** — SDSS/BOSS/XQ-100 era through the DESI DR1 P1D analyses (optimal-estimator and FFT measurements, cosmological analysis).
- **3D statistics & full-shape** — a strengthening sub-theme: the DESI DR2 Lyα full-shape validation (Herbold et al. 2026), P3D theory, and the IGM-thermal-state impact on P3D (Šoltinský et al. 2026).
- **EFT & hybrid modelling** — analytic compression of the forest EFT (Karaçaylı et al. 2026), simulation+EFT hybrid field model (de Belsunce et al. 2026).
- **Emulation & compression** — ForestFlow, information compression of 1D correlations, lognormal synthetic spectra.
- **Estimator methodology & systematics** — quadratic/optimal vs. FFT estimators, BAL masking (Martini et al. 2025), continuum-fitting systematics, high-resolution small-scale forest (PRIYA vs. XQ-100/KODIAQ-SQUAD).
- **Fundamental physics via the forest** — neutrino mass, dark matter (§5).

**Activity level: very high and current.** This is the live working area.

## 2. IGM astrophysics: thermal state & reionization — *primary focus (reinforced this cycle)*

The astrophysics half of the forest program, and the branch with the strongest new-addition signal this summer. To use the forest at percent-level precision you must model the medium: its thermal history, its opacity, and the relics reionization leaves behind.

**Scope & sub-themes:**
- **Thermal state & temperature–density relation** *(new emphasis)* — T–ρ theory (McQuinn & Upton Sanderbeck 2016; Hui, Gnedin & Zhang 1997), measurements from the power spectrum (Walther et al. 2019) and Voigt-profile decomposition (Hiss et al. 2018), photoheating and UV-background synthesis models (Puchwein et al. 2015), and large-scale temperature/intensity fluctuations from patchy reionization (McQuinn et al. 2011). The Šoltinský et al. (2026) P3D paper ties this directly to forest cosmology.
- **Opacity, optical depth & mean free path** — direct MFP measurements at 5 < z < 6, Lyman-limit MFP in Sherwood-Relics, late-reionization opacity fluctuations.
- **Late reionization & relics** — reionization ending at z ≈ 5.3 (XQR-30), neutral-hydrogen islands, damping-wing features, high-z forest autocorrelations, and reionization relics persisting to z ≈ 2 in the eBOSS Lyα correlations (Zheng et al. 2026); CMB reionization observables (Hu & Holder 2003) as complementary context.
- **UV background & ionizing sources**; **QSO continuum reconstruction** (now fused with §4); **HI & DLA** systems; **Lyβ forest** and Lyα×Lyβ cross-power (Wilson et al. 2022).

**Activity level: high and rising — the clearest new cluster of the May–August additions.**

## 3. Large-scale structure & spectroscopic surveys — *secondary focus*

**Scope & sub-themes:**
- **BAO & full-shape clustering** — DESI DR1/DR2 Lyα BAO, DR2 dynamical dark energy, full-shape analyses; high-z quasar clustering with DESI DR2 (Charles et al. 2026).
- **Quasar samples & target selection** — DESI/SDSS quasar catalogs, miniJPAS/gradient-boosting quasar selection.
- **Void cosmology** — finders, measurements, modelling; a mature secondary probe.
- **Techniques** — covariance estimation, Fisher forecasting, 2PCF/power-spectrum estimators, scattering transforms (Cheng & Ménard guide).
- **Simulations & codes** — Sherwood/Sherwood-Relics, PRIYA, Quijote, CAMELS, Abacus; the infrastructure feeding §1 and §2. Galaxy formation in modified gravity and assembly bias appear as context.
- **Surveys** — DESI dominates; SDSS/eBOSS/BOSS legacy; WEAVE-QSO/PFS/4MOST as the next forest generation.

**Activity level: high for DESI DR2 material; evergreen for techniques.**

## 4. Quasar spectra, continua & AGN — *rising secondary focus (new this cycle)*

A newly coherent astronomy-flavored branch, motivated by the QSO-continuum systematic in forest cosmology but reaching into AGN astrophysics proper.

**Scope & sub-themes:** Gaussian-process generative models of quasar spectra (Eilers et al. 2022, with the GP latent-variable model literature behind it); transformer-based probabilistic reconstruction of AGN spectra (AGNFormer, 2026); SDSS Reverberation Mapping key results (Shen et al. 2024); type-2 quasar demographics (Reyes et al. 2008); Balmer-absorption AGN and JWST little red dots (Shangguan et al. 2026).

**Recommendation guidance:** quasar-continuum modelling, quasar spectral variability/physics, and data-driven spectral models are in-scope — especially anything usable for forest continuum fitting or DESI/SDSS quasar samples. Pure accretion-disk theory or single-object AGN studies remain low priority.

**Activity level: rising — treat as a genuine (astronomy) interest, not a fringe.**

## 5. Dark matter & neutrinos from small-scale structure — *secondary focus (fused with #1)*

Warm dark matter constraints from the forest (Viel, Villasenor, Baur), free-streaming at the smallest scales (Iršič et al. 2024), ultralight/fuzzy axion dark matter (Rogers & Peiris 2021), neutrino masses from the forest power spectrum, nature-of-DM reviews. ## 6. Machine learning & statistical inference — *secondary focus, method-specific*

The ML branch is substantial and active, and it now has a clear organizing project: a **self-supervised transformer / foundation model for quasar spectra** (the "QGPT" collection, the most active ML collection this summer). The interest is in *methods*, and a good methods paper is welcome whether or not it mentions astronomy — but the methods themselves are specific, not generic.

**Current ML method interests (recommend papers on these, astro-applied or not):**
- **Transformer interpretability & mechanistic interpretability** — attention attribution and its critiques (Chefer et al.; Abnar & Zuidema; "Attention is (not) Explanation"), sparse autoencoders and feature discovery, representation editing / null-space projection (INLP, AlphaEdit), representation-similarity measures (partial distance correlation).
- **Self-supervised & contrastive representation learning** — SimCLR, BYOL, MoCo, iBOT-style pretraining, especially for sequential/spectral/time-series data (TS2Vec, ContiFormer, universal time-series representation learning); tokenization schemes for continuous scientific data.
- **Foundation models for scientific data** — architecture, pretraining, and adaptation of multimodal/spectral foundation models (AION-1, AstroCLIP, SpecPT, SpecCLIP, universal spectral tokenization); LLM-style pretraining insight that transfers to spectra.
- **Probabilistic generative models & inference** — normalizing flows and stochastic interpolants/diffusion, Gaussian-process latent-variable models, latent factor models, simulation-based inference, information-theoretic summary-statistic evaluation (MINE, sufficiency/complementarity), Bayesian deep learning and uncertainty quantification.
- **Sampling & Bayesian computation** — HMC/microcanonical Langevin (Robnik & Seljak-style), nested sampling, sampling neural-network posteriors.
- **Deep-learning theory** — generalization, double descent, benign overfitting (Belkin, Bartlett, Nakkiran).
- **Emulation, neural operators, symbolic regression, denoising** (Noise2Noise-style self-supervised denoising) — the surrogate-modelling and data-cleaning toolkit.

**Recommendation guidance:**
- An ML paper squarely on one of the threads above earns a Tier 1/Tier 2 slot on its own merits — do not require an astronomy hook. ML papers *with* a forest/spectroscopy application (SBI for P1D, ML continuum fitting, DLA/BAL finders, spectral foundation models) are the strongest matches of all.
- What stays out is ML *outside* these threads: benchmark-chasing vision/NLP applications, prompt engineering, recommender systems, generic MLOps/systems work, and RL beyond what informs LLM-style training (PPO/GRPO-adjacent work is marginally in).
- Relative to the 2026.05 profile the *overall* ML share of the digest should be somewhat smaller in favor of astronomy — trim by raising the quality bar within these threads, not by excluding them.
