## Tier 1 — Highly relevant

### [No way ouτ: Epoch of Reionization Observations Do not Support Large Values of the Optical Depth to Reionization](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26373v1)

Paulo Montero-Camacho, Yi Mao
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Recent DESI DR2 BAO + CMB analyses prefer dynamical dark energy over ΛCDM at ~3σ, but that preference weakens to ≲2σ if large-scale CMB polarization is dropped — at the cost of pushing the reionization optical depth τ up to ~0.09. The authors test whether astrophysical reionization data can independently pin τ, using a physically motivated Gompertzian (asymmetric) reionization history and adding robust IGM constraints — quasar damping wings and Lyα/Lyβ dark-pixel neutral-fraction measurements — to CMB (minus low-ℓ EE) + BAO. They derive τ = 0.067 ± 0.011, consistent with the polarization-based value, while the w₀wₐCDM preference over ΛCDM survives at ≳2σ. The modest τ increase is also enough to remove the spurious pull toward negative neutrino masses (Σmν peaks near 0.1 eV).

Direct hit on the profile's fused IGM/reionization core (quasar damping wings, dark-pixel optical-depth measurements) meeting DESI-era BAO and dark-energy inference — exactly the "reionization observable as a cosmological lever" intersection the library is built around.

### [An efficient one-loop EFTofLSS framework for Vainshtein-screened Horndeski gravity](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26945v1)

Stan Verhoeve, Dani de Boe, Gen Ye et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Extends the public one-loop EFTofLSS code PyBird to modified gravity: quasi-static, Vainshtein-screened luminal Horndeski models (in both EFTofDE and covariant forms) plus nDGP. It replaces PyBird's Green's-function machinery with a direct ODE solver for the exact time-dependent perturbation kernels, improving efficiency while dropping the Einstein–de Sitter approximation — which they show can bias the redshift-space quadrupole by up to ~4σ at BOSS precision for extreme modified-gravity parameters. Applied to Planck CMB + BOSS full-shape + DESI DR2 BAO, the pipeline constrains the EFTofDE braiding and running amplitudes (c_B, c_M), finding consistency with GR at 95% CL, with full-shape data substantially tightening c_M.

The interest file explicitly tracks EFTofLSS and PyBird-style full-shape analyses on DESI/BOSS data; this is a ready-to-use extension of that exact inference machinery, plus a concrete warning that the standard EdS kernel approximation is not automatically safe once you leave ΛCDM.

## Tier 2 — Adjacent / useful context

### [Emulating the complex galactic-scale orbital dynamics of LISA massive black hole pairs with normalizing flows](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26125v1)

Pedro R. Capelo, Carlos Moreno Martinez, Nodens Koren et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.GA | also: astro-ph.CO

Trains conditional normalizing flows to emulate the galactic-scale orbital decay of inspiralling massive-black-hole pairs, learning the distribution of the binary-formation time τ_d conditioned on MBH and host/bar properties from a large suite of semi-analytical orbital integrations. The surrogate reproduces the simulations' decay-time distributions with calibrated (probability–probability-verified) uncertainties while running orders of magnitude faster, and is then applied to TNG50 galaxy populations to infer MBH-binary formation timescales across cosmic time — finding that stellar bars measurably suppress binary formation. The authors frame it explicitly as a proof of concept for bridging expensive, well-understood small-scale physics to population scale.

A clean template for the profile's rising-primary methods interest: conditional normalizing flows as a simulation-based surrogate/emulator — the same tool class (flows, emulation) the library collects for forest/LSS inference, demonstrated here on a structurally identical "well-understood-but-intractable-at-scale" problem.

### [Shear-kSZ: A New Estimator for the Matter-Electron Power Spectrum from kSZ Tomography and Weak Lensing](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27149v1)

Boryana Hadzhiyska
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO | also: astro-ph.GA

Proposes a new estimator that correlates the kinematic-SZ field with the reconstructed line-of-sight velocity field and the weak-lensing convergence, isolating the matter–electron cross-power spectrum P_me(k) for the full matter distribution rather than around a specific biased tracer. This gives direct access to the baryonic suppression S(k) of the matter power spectrum — one of the dominant astrophysical systematics for Stage-IV cosmic shear — and, by its parity structure, CMB foregrounds cancel. Validated analytically against simulations at percent level, it forecasts a 16σ measurement (≈1% on S(k)) over f_sky=0.2 with Simons Observatory-like CMB noise, DESI-like LRG velocity reconstruction, and LSST-like sources, with a ~10σ detection already plausible from early LSST data.

Baryonic feedback suppression of P(k) is a first-order systematic for the DESI/LSST-era LSS cosmology the profile centers on; a new, foreground-immune handle on P_me(k) is a directly usable measurement target for the precision-cosmology methods shelf.

*No figures extracted for 2607.27149 (forecast/derivation paper; the S(k) forecast is fully conveyed by the text).*

### [Searching for signatures of inflationary massive fields in DESI Imaging data and Stage-V galaxy surveys](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27175v1)

Walter Riquelme, Santiago Avila, Lucas Pinol et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Searches for primordial non-Gaussianity from massive fields during inflation via a scale-dependent bias (∝ f_NL,Δ k^{Δ−2}) in the angular clustering of DESI imaging targets. With aggressive systematics treatment there is no signal; a less-aggressive treatment shows a non-robust hint of local f_NL≈27 and beyond-local Δ≈0.91, which the authors present only as a showcase since it tracks residual systematics. They then forecast Stage-V spectroscopic surveys (WST, MUST, Spec-S5) using Lyman-break galaxies, finding σ(Δ)≈0.17–0.50, and derive a relation between the detectability of Δ and the achievable σ(f_NL^loc).

DESI clustering, PNG via scale-dependent bias, and next-generation Lyman-break-galaxy spectroscopic surveys all sit in the profile's LSS/survey wheelhouse; the Stage-V/LBG forecasts in particular preview the WEAVE-QSO/PFS-adjacent survey landscape the user is tracking.

*No figures extracted for 2607.27175 (constraints and forecasts are adequately captured in text).*

### [The FRB–Galaxy Overdensity Cross-Correlation Statistic in Dispersion Space](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26135v1)

Ryan Raikman, Haochen Wang, Kiyoshi Masui et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Develops the f×g statistic — the angular cross-power spectrum between FRB counts binned by dispersion measure and galaxy density binned by redshift — and shows it is strictly more informative than the simpler D×g DM–galaxy statistic, which it recovers as a DM-moment. Slicing in both DM and redshift separates the clustering of free electrons from that of FRB sources, letting one probe the baryon distribution even without FRB host redshifts. Fisher forecasts for CHIME×DESI-BGS (1,600 FRBs) and CHORD×Euclid (20,000 FRBs) give S/N≈12 and 54, and measure the baryon-feedback clustering cutoff scale k_cut to 26% and 14% precision respectively.

Another baryons/feedback cross-correlation probe tied to DESI/Euclid galaxy samples and Fisher forecasting — squarely in the profile's LSS-techniques and baryon-distribution interests, and methodologically a cousin of the Lyα cross-correlation program.

*No figures extracted for 2607.26135 (the forecast S/N and k_cut precisions are the key results and are stated in text).*

### [Radio Recombination Line Contamination in Post-Reionization 21 cm Intensity Mapping](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26180v1)

Pip Petersen, Yakov Faerman, Matthew McQuinn
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Quantifies contamination of post-reionization 21cm intensity-mapping power spectra by the many radio recombination lines (RRLs) that redshift into the band. A conservative extrapolated upper-bound model predicts large contamination at z≲2, but the authors' empirically-calibrated models put it orders of magnitude lower, with carbon RRLs subdominant. The contamination is highly oscillatory in wavenumber — a shape that could in principle bias BAO distance measurements — but they find the induced shift in the BAO peak is at least two orders of magnitude below what matters for percent-precision cosmology.

21cm intensity mapping as a BAO probe is an adjacent-but-tracked topic in the profile, and this is a clean systematics-vetting result for exactly those IM-BAO measurements — from a McQuinn-group IGM analysis.

*No figures extracted for 2607.26180 (systematics forecast; the "BAO shift is negligible" conclusion needs no plot here).*

### [J-PAS & FLAMINGO: Cosmic voids and void galaxies in the gravitational landscape of photometric surveys](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26932v1)

J. A. Mansour, B. McCarthy, L. J. Liivamägi et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.CO

Tests whether cosmic voids and their imprint on galaxy properties survive photometric-redshift errors in J-PAS-like data, using FLAMINGO mocks at z=0.3. Comparing an ideal mock to one with J-PAS-like redshift errors, a quasi-gravitational-potential field plus a watershed algorithm recovers dynamically dominant voids with excellent size/shape agreement (occupying ~63% of the thresholded potential volume), the main degradation being interior contamination by galaxies scattered from dense regions. In both mocks, void galaxies show lower stellar masses, bluer colors, and enhanced star formation relative to equal-mass galaxies in high-density environments.

Void cosmology is a well-developed secondary probe in the profile, and J-PAS specifically appears in the interest file; this is a concrete, tested recipe for doing void science with the photometric-redshift surveys the user follows.

*No figures extracted for 2607.26932 (methodology-and-mocks paper; the void-recovery result is clear from the summary).*

### [Constraining the shape of dark matter haloes using only starlight II. Tests of the technique with objects of known gravitational potential](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27001v1)

Jorge Sanchez Almeida, Angel R. Plastino, Sergio Guerra Arencibia et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.GA | also: astro-ph.CO

Validates the Eddington Inversion Method (EIM) tool, a photometry-only diagnostic that tests whether an assumed cored or cuspy gravitational potential is physically consistent with an observed stellar surface-density profile — a stellar core inside a cuspy NFW halo requires an unphysical negative phase-space distribution function. Applied to 21 globular clusters it rejects NFW cusps in 71% of cases and favors cored Schuster–Plummer models with accurate core radii; on dwarf spheroidals it reproduces classical dynamical results (Sculptor/Fornax cored, Draco cusp-compatible); and tests on EDGE and FIRE simulations confirm it reliably rejects cusps when the truth is cored. Because it needs only photometry rather than long spectroscopic campaigns, it is built for upcoming large photometric surveys.

Cusp-vs-core constraints on the nature of dark matter from small-scale structure are a listed secondary focus; this offers a cheap, survey-scalable alternative to spectroscopic dynamics, complementary to the forest-based small-scale dark-matter constraints the user tracks.

*No figures extracted for 2607.27001 (validation paper; the cusp-rejection statistics are the takeaway and are given in text).*

## Tier 3 — Outside my area but notable

### [An interstellar energetic and non-aqueous pathway to peptide formation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26814v1)

Alfred Thomas Hopkinson, Ann Mary Wilson, Joe Pitfield et al.
**Primary category:** astro-ph.SR | also: astro-ph.EP, astro-ph.GA

Shows experimentally that peptide bonds can form under space-like conditions without liquid water. Irradiating isotopically labelled glycine embedded in interstellar-ice analogues with protons at cryogenic temperatures produces glycylglycine — the simplest dipeptide — along with (deuterated and non-deuterated) water as a by-product, with peptide-bond formation confirmed by infrared spectroscopy and high-resolution mass spectrometry. This establishes a non-aqueous, radiation-driven route to the building blocks of proteins that could operate in the cold interstellar medium and be inherited by forming planetary systems.

Well outside the profile, but a genuinely surprising origins-of-life result: peptides assembling in irradiated interstellar ice with no liquid water challenges the aqueous-centric picture of prebiotic chemistry — the kind of broadly significant finding worth knowing as a scientist.
